# Scavenger Rating Addon

**Scavenger Rating Addon** is a Fabric mod that brings global speedrun leaderboards to the Scavenger mod!

Have you ever wanted to know who the fastest Scavenger player is? Now you can! This addon connects to a global database and tracks the fastest completion times for every single item and modifier combination. 

### 🏆 Features
- **Global Leaderboards:** See the top 10 players for any category.
- **Beautiful In-Game UI:** Easily filter runs by item or modifier directly inside Minecraft.
- **Strict Anti-Cheat:** Runs are strictly monitored. If a player uses Creative mode, Spectator mode, or executes any cheat/operator commands during their run, their time is instantly invalidated.
- **Multi-Language:** Fully translated into English and Russian.

### ⚙️ Requirements
- **Minecraft 1.21.11**
- **Fabric Loader** & **Fabric API**
- **Scavenger mod** (Required dependency)
